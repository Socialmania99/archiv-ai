Notion

Send chats straight into a Notion page. 1) Create an internal integration at notion.so/my-integrations and copy its secret. 2) Open the page where chats should go, click ••• › Connections, add your integration. 3) Paste both below. The token stays in this browser only.

Beginner guide: connect Notion step by step
  1. Have a Notion account. Free is fine. Sign in at notion.so.
  2. Make a page to hold your chats. In Notion click + New page in the sidebar, name it AI Chats. Leave it empty.
  3. Create an integration (a key that lets Archiv AI write). Open notion.so/profile/integrations (older link: notion.so/my-integrations). Click New integration, name it Archiv AI, pick your workspace, type Internal, then Save.
  4. Allow it to write. On the integration's Capabilities tab keep Read content, Insert content and Update content ticked.
  5. Copy the secret. On the Configuration tab click Show then Copy next to Internal Integration Secret. It starts with ntn_ or secret_. Treat it like a password.
  6. Share the page with the integration. Open your AI Chats page, click ••• (top right) › Connections › search Archiv AI › Confirm. Skip this and Notion answers “object not found”.
  7. Copy the page link. On that page click Share › Copy link.
  8. Paste both below. Secret into Integration secret, link into Parent page link or ID.
  9. Choose how chats are saved. Separate pages: each chat is a sub-page. Rows in a database: Archiv AI creates a table Archiv AI chats with Site, Tags, Date, Messages and URL columns.
  10. Click Connect and test. Your browser asks permission to reach api.notion.com: click Allow. Status turns to “Connected to …”.
  11. Send a chat. Open a chat, click Archiv AI › Export this chat › in the Inbox click Export…, pick Notion in the format menu, then Send to Notion. Click Open in Notion when done.

Problems? “Could not find page / Connections”: redo step 6. “Token”/401: copy the secret again (step 5). Nothing happens on Allow: click Connect again. Your secret stays in this browser; it is never synced or sent anywhere except api.notion.com. Disconnect any time with the button below and delete the integration in Notion to revoke it fully.
