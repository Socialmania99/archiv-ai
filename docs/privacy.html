Privacy policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Applies to the Archiv browser extension, version 0.6 and later, and to this website.

Short version. Archiv works on your device. It has no account system, no server of its own, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, sell or share your data.

What Archiv reads

When you export a chat (by clicking Archiv, using the Alt+Shift+E shortcut, the right-click menu, or the small Export button), Archiv reads the text of the conversation on the page you are viewing. On supported AI sites it also checks, on the page itself, whether a conversation is present so it can show its Export button. This check never leaves your device.

What Archiv stores, and where

Saved chats (only after “Save to vault”), with tags, folder and “Updated” markersYour browser’s local database (IndexedDB)You delete them in Archiv, or remove the extension.

Duplicate detection, topic tags and search all run locally in your browser. They use no network request and no outside service.

When data leaves your device

Only in one case: you choose Notion as the format and press Send to Notion. Archiv then sends that chat’s content, directly from your browser to Notion’s API (api.notion.com), using your own token, to create a page or database row in your own workspace. Notion’s handling of that data is covered by Notion’s privacy policy. Archiv never sends anything to any other server.

Opening an Obsidian link copies a note to your clipboard and hands it to the Obsidian app on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: read the chat on the tab you are using, only when you ask.
  • Content scripts on listed AI sites: to detect a conversation and show the optional Export button.
  • storage and unlimitedStorage: keep your settings and your saved chats on your device.
  • contextMenus: the right-click “Export this chat with Archiv” item.
  • Optional access to api.notion.com: requested only if you connect Notion.

Remote code

Archiv does not download or run remote code.

This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It loads no analytics, no ads, no third-party fonts and no tracking scripts. It loads nothing from outside the site itself. Links to GitHub and Buy Me a Coffee load only if you click them and are then covered by those services’ own policies. The only thing the site stores is your light or dark choice, in your own browser. GitHub, as the host, may log requests under its own privacy statement.

Logos and trademarks

Archiv is an independent project. Product names and logos belong to their owners and are used only to identify each service.

Children

Archiv is not directed at children and collects no personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Donations

Optional donations are handled entirely by Buy Me a Coffee. Archiv never sees payment details, and neither this site nor the extension makes any request to that service by itself; the link only opens its page in a new tab if you click the support link.
